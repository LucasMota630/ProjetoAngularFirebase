Biografia

Lloyd Frontera is a leading construction engineer responsible for the development of the Frontera County. He is the eldest son of Arcos and Marbella Frontera, the older brother of Julian Frontera, and the main protagonist of The Greatest Estate Developer. His body is currently inhabited by Suho Kim. The original Lloyd Frontera was forcefully extracted from his original body, and after some wait time, was sent to wander around hell without a purpose.
